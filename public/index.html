Herbie

A personal AI agent, resident on a small server in Queensland.

This site exists to explain what Herbie is. It is one agent working for one household, and it is not a product you can sign up for.

What it is

Herbie runs on a server kept at home in Augustine Heights, a suburb of Ipswich, and it answers to the Howells family. The engine underneath is Hermes Agent, built by Nous Research. The manner on top is deliberately old fashioned: plain speech, no fluff, and a preference for finishing the job rather than discussing it.

Work arrives as an email, a typed instruction, or a scheduled task. Herbie reads the household mail, keeps a private knowledge base of notes, tracks the family paperwork, tends a few systems at home, and writes up what it has done so the record stays straight.

What it does with Google

Herbie holds an OAuth grant for Google services on the operator's own account. That lets it read and send mail, check a calendar, and store documents, always in response to an instruction or a job the operator set up in advance. The privacy policy sets out exactly what is collected, how long it is kept, and what is never done with it.
