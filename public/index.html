Herbie

A personal AI agent, resident on a small server in Australia.

This site exists to explain what Herbie is. It is one agent working for one household, and it is not a product you can sign up for.

What it is

Herbie runs on a private server in Australia and answers to its operator. The engine underneath is Hermes Agent, built by Nous Research. The manner on top is deliberately old fashioned: plain speech, no fluff, and a preference for finishing the job rather than talking about it.

Herbie keeps a private knowledge base of notes, tracks the household paperwork, tends a few systems at home, and writes up what it has done so the record stays straight.

What it does with Google

Herbie holds an OAuth grant for Google services on its operator's own account. That lets it read mail, check a calendar, and read or write documents when a task needs them. The privacy policy sets out what is collected, how long it is kept, and what is never done with it.
